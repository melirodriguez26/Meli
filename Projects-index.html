Plant Bud

In 2025, I participated in a project to create a website about plant care, where I worked as a project manager and on the design side. During that time, I developed leadership and teamwork skills, and I also discovered a new interest in design thanks to the training I received. In 2026, I continued to contribute to the project’s resources and design, presenting the project and carrying out a rebranding of the color scheme and logo. All of this effort led us to develop a web app that made it to EXPO TECH and Shark Tank, representing our center alongside six others from El Salvador. This tool not only taught users about plants but also allowed them to view gardens in 3D and offered features that enriched the user experience.
